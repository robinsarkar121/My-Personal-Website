Who I Am

Hi! I'm Robin Sarkar, originally from Sylhet, Bangladesh, and now based in Queens, NY. I'm a computer science student at LaGuardia Community College. Right now I'm deep in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript fundamentals, with React, Node, and the rest of the stack on the road ahead. I've also worked with C, C++, Java, and Python through my coursework.

I like building things that are simple, accessible, and a little bit playful. During my internship at Plum Alley, I built a web scraper and a relationship-mapping platform to help the team find and connect with investors, and I currently work as a Student Technology Mentor, helping students and faculty solve their tech problems. My long-term goal is to build a tech startup that combines innovation and problem-solving to create meaningful impact.

Outside of code: gaming, soccer, fishing, hiking, and traveling. 🎮
